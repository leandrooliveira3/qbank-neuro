Reference geometry and navigation data — QBank-Neuro

reference-network.json, reference-surfaces.json and the associated
public/neuro3d/open-anatomy/reference-surfaces.bin are adaptations of the
male atlas meshes distributed by Anatria3D, derived from Z-Anatomy and
BodyParts3D / DBCLS. These adapted data remain CC BY-SA 4.0.
